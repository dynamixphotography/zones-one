    Under the foot

    A base that wraps around you.

    The sole is built to pull heat away from the foot and keep it vented and temperature regulated. Under pressure it molds to take your shape, creates the mold, then supports the foot. The spec drawing shows it as encapsulated zones in side cross-section.

    Why it matters

    Adaptability changes the game.

    01

    Your foot sets the shape.

    No two feet are the same. AdaptStep is drawn to form a custom mold around yours instead of asking you to settle for a fixed one.

    02

    The shape stays put.

    The drawings call for a locking pressure, so the fit you set is the fit you keep. Mold retention is part of the design.

    03

    One shoe. Every foot.

    When a shoe adapts, the shoe stops being the limit. You decide the fit. The shoe follows.
